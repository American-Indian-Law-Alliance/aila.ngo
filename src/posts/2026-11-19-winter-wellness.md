How do we care for ourselves and one another through the winter? Join the American Indian Law Alliance (AILA) online **Tuesday, December 15, 2026, from 7:00 to 8:45 p.m. Eastern**. Haudenosaunee knowledge keepers and an Oneida physician will share winter teachings and take questions. Indigenous community members and allies are welcome.

Betty Lyons (Onondaga Nation, Snipe Clan), AILA’s president and executive director, will host. The evening will open and close with the Thanksgiving Address. Bring your questions about traditional foods and medicines, Indigenous women’s health, and the winter practices you use at home.

## Meet the speakers

- **Jeanne Shenandoah** (Onondaga Nation, Eel Clan) is an Elder and traditional medicine keeper whose work includes midwifery. She will share teachings about white pine tea and its preparation.
- **Haiwhagai'i Jake Edwards** (Onondaga Nation, Eel Clan), a community educator, will speak about how Haudenosaunee people have cared for one another through the cold months and how families carry that knowledge today.
- **Dr. Brian Thompson** (Oneida Nation, Wolf Clan), a physician at SUNY Upstate Medical University and an AILA board member, will discuss winter foods, rest, and staying active through the season.
